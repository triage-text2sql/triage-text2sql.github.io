TRIAGE: Schema-Grounded Challenging Data Synthesis to Build Failure-aware Agentic Text-to-SQL System

Under submission

Abstract

Text-to-SQL systems remain brittle on enterprise-grade schemas, where the hardest queries require traversing long chains of foreign-key joins and disambiguating look-alike columns. We argue this gap stems from a data bottleneck: existing training and synthesis pipelines under-represent the structurally deep regions of a schema graph. We introduce SPLICE, a schema-grounded method that recasts hard-data generation as coverage expansion over a schema's foreign-key graph, deterministically composing query semantics from verified sub-paths to produce hallucination-free, path-complete examples. Building on this data, we present TRIAGE, an inference-time agentic pipeline that distills a compact, empirically observed taxonomy of failure modes into dedicated specialist agents, reconciled by an execution-grounded merger and refined through a calibrated LLM-as-judge feedback loop. Without any benchmark-specific training, TRIAGE attains 74.1% execution accuracy on the BIRD development set and generalizes across five Text-to-SQL benchmarks.
